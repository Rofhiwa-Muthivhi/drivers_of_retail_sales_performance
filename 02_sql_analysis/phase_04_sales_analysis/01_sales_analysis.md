# Sales Trends Analysis

## Objective
The purpose of this analysis was to identify sales trends over time and understand seasonal business performance using monthly, yearly, and weekly sales data.


## 1. Monthly Sales Trends

### Analysis
Monthly sales were analyzed to determine which months generated the highest revenue.

### Key Findings
- **July recorded the highest sales performance, followed by April and June.**
- **January recorded the lowest sales performance.**
- Sales varied across months, indicating **seasonal demand patterns**

### Business Insight
Understanding monthly performance will help the business improve:
- Inventory planning
- Staffing decisions
- Promotional strategies


## 2. Yearly Sales Trends

### Analysis
Yearly sales were analyzed to assess changes in business performance over time and identify overall sales trends.

### Key Findings
- Sales increased from **2010 to 2011.**
- **2011 recorded the highest yearly sales.**
- 2012 reported lower total sales than 2011.
- Further investigation was conducted to determine whether the decline reflected actual business performance or incomplete data.

### Business Insight
Year-over-year sales comparisons should be interpreted carefully to avoid misleading conclusions.


## 3. Dataset Completeness Validation

### Analysis
A validation check was performed to ensure that yearly sales comparisons were fair and accurate.

### Key Findings
- **2011 contained a full 52 weeks of data**
- **2010 and 2012 contained fewer than 52 weeks of data**

### Business Insight
The lower sales recorded in 2012 do not necessarily indicate a decline in business performance because the year contains incomplete data.


## 4. Weekly Sales Trends

### Analysis
Weekly sales were analyzed to identify recurring sales patterns.

### Key Findings
- Sales showed **recurring weekly fluctuations**
- Certain weeks consistently performed better than others

### Business Insight
Sales are seasonal and are not consistent throughout the year, supporting better operational and promotional planning.


## 5. Top Performing Weeks

### Analysis
Top-performing weeks were identified using average weekly sales.

### Key Findings
- Week 52 recorded the highest average sales performance.
- Weeks 48, 51, and 50 were also among the strongest performing weeks.
- Most of the top performing weeks occurred near the end of the year.

### Business Insight
Customer spending increases usually during the final weeks of the year, making this period important for sales growth and revenue generation. This happens because of the following:
- Festive shopping
- Increased consumer spending
- Promotional activity


## Overall Conclusion

The analysis showed that sales change throughout the year and follow clear seasonal patterns.

### Major Findings
- July was the highest-performing month.
- 2011 recorded the highest annual sales.
- Weeks 48–52 generated the strongest sales performance.
- Yearly sales results should be checked against data completeness before drawing conclusions.

Overall, understanding these sales patterns can help the business make better decisions about inventory, staffing, promotions, and future planning.
