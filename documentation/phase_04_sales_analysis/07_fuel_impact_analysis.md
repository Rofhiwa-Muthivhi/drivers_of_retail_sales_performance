# Fuel Price Impact Analysis

## Objective

The purpose of this analysis was to evaluate whether fuel prices influence retail sales performance and determine whether changes in transport costs affect customer spending behavior.


## Sales Performance by Fuel Price Category

### Analysis

Sales data was joined with economic data from the features table using store and date. Fuel prices were grouped into three categories:

* Low Fuel Price (< 3.0)
* Moderate Fuel Price (3.0–3.7)
* High Fuel Price (> 3.7)

Average sales were then compared across the three groups.

### Findings

* Moderate fuel price periods generated the highest average sales.
* High fuel price periods recorded slightly lower average sales.
* Differences between the fuel price categories were relatively small.
* Transaction activity was present across all fuel price categories.

### Business Insight

The results suggest that fuel prices may have some influence on retail sales performance. However, the differences between categories were relatively small, indicating that fuel prices alone may not be a major driver of customer spending behavior.


## Data Quality Review

### Analysis

A review of the fuel price field was performed before the analysis to ensure the data was suitable for comparison.

### Findings

* No missing or non-numeric values were identified.
* Fuel price values were already stored in a usable format.
* No additional data cleaning was required.

### Business Insight

The fuel price data was clean and ready for analysis, allowing the results to be generated without additional transformation or correction.


## Fuel Price Range Review

### Analysis

The minimum and maximum fuel prices were reviewed to understand the spread of values within the dataset.

### Findings

* Fuel prices varied across the analysis period.
* The observed range provided sufficient variation for category-based analysis.

### Business Insight

Reviewing the fuel price range helped ensure that the category thresholds reflected meaningful differences in fuel price levels.


## Overall Conclusion

The analysis showed only small differences in sales performance across fuel price categories.

### Key Findings

* Moderate fuel price periods generated the highest average sales.
* High fuel price periods recorded slightly lower average sales.
* Differences between categories were relatively small.
* No data quality issues were identified in the fuel price field.

Overall, the findings suggest that fuel prices may influence retail sales performance to some extent, but the relationship appears weaker than other economic factors examined in this project.
