# Sales Trends Analysis

## Objective
The purpose of this analysis was to identify sales trends over time and understand seasonal business performance using monthly, yearly, and weekly sales data.

---

## 1. Monthly Sales Trends

### Analysis
Monthly sales were analyzed to determine which months generated the highest revenue.

### Key Findings
- **July recorded the highest sales performance**
- Sales varied across months, indicating **seasonal demand patterns**

### Business Insight
Understanding high-performing months helps businesses improve:
- Inventory planning
- Staffing decisions
- Promotional strategies

---

## 2. Yearly Sales Trends

### Analysis
Yearly sales were evaluated to determine whether business performance was growing or declining.

### Key Findings
- Sales increased from **2010 to 2011**
- **2011 recorded the highest yearly sales**
- Although **2012 showed lower sales**, further validation was required

### Business Insight
Year-over-year sales comparisons should be interpreted carefully to avoid misleading conclusions.

---

## 3. Dataset Completeness Validation

### Analysis
A validation check was conducted to confirm whether yearly comparisons were fair.

### Key Findings
- **2011 contained a full 52 weeks of data**
- **2010 and 2012 were incomplete years**

### Business Insight
The lower sales in 2012 should not be interpreted as business decline due to incomplete data availability.

---

## 4. Weekly Sales Trends

### Analysis
Weekly sales were analyzed to identify recurring sales patterns.

### Key Findings
- Sales showed **recurring weekly fluctuations**
- Certain weeks consistently performed better than others

### Business Insight
Sales are seasonal and vary throughout the year, supporting better operational and promotional planning.

---

## 5. Peak Weekly Performance

### Analysis
Top-performing weeks were identified using average weekly sales.

### Key Findings
- **Weeks 48–52 generated the highest sales**
- **Week 52 recorded the strongest performance**

### Business Insight
The business experiences strong **year-end seasonality**, likely driven by:
- Festive shopping
- Increased consumer spending
- Promotional activity

---

## Overall Conclusion

The analysis revealed clear **seasonal sales behavior** across months, years, and weeks.

### Major Findings
- **July emerged as the highest-performing month**
- **2011 recorded the strongest yearly sales performance**
- **Weeks 48–52 consistently generated the highest sales activity**
- Yearly sales comparisons require validation when datasets contain incomplete periods

Overall, retail sales fluctuate over time, highlighting the importance of seasonality in forecasting, inventory planning, and business decision-making.# Sales Trends Analysis
