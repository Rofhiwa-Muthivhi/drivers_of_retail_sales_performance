# Store Performance Analysis

## Objective
The purpose of this analysis was to evaluate store performance, identify top and bottom-performing stores, and determine whether store type and size influence sales performance.

---

## 1. Top-Performing Stores

### Analysis
Stores were ranked based on total sales to identify the strongest revenue contributors.

### Key Findings
- **Store 20 recorded the highest sales performance**
- A small group of stores generated significantly higher revenue than others
- Top-performing stores contributed a substantial portion of overall sales

### Business Insight
High-performing stores may provide valuable insights into successful business strategies, customer demand, and operational effectiveness.

---

## 2. Bottom-Performing Stores

### Analysis
Low-performing stores were identified to understand weaker sales performance.

### Key Findings
- Some stores generated significantly lower revenue compared to top performers
- A noticeable performance gap exists between high and low-performing stores

### Business Insight
Underperforming stores may require operational improvements, targeted support, or market evaluation.

---

## 3. Store Type Performance

### Analysis
Sales performance was compared across store types (**A, B, and C**).

### Key Findings
- **Type A stores generated the highest total sales**
- **Type B stores showed moderate performance**
- **Type C stores contributed the lowest sales**

### Business Insight
Store type appears to influence overall sales performance, with larger store formats contributing more revenue.

---

## 4. Average Sales by Store Type

### Analysis
Average sales per store type were calculated to allow fair comparison across different store counts.

### Key Findings
- **Type A stores achieved the highest average sales per store**
- **Type B stores performed moderately**
- **Type C stores recorded the lowest average sales**

### Business Insight
Even after adjusting for the number of stores, **Type A stores consistently outperformed other store categories**, suggesting stronger operational performance.

---

## 5. Store Size and Sales Performance

### Analysis
The relationship between store size and sales performance was evaluated.

### Key Findings
- Larger stores generally generated higher sales
- Store size appeared to positively influence revenue performance
- However, size alone did not fully explain differences in sales performance

### Business Insight
Although larger stores tend to perform better, additional factors such as location, customer traffic, and product mix may also influence sales.

---

## 6. Store Size Segmentation Analysis

### Analysis
Stores were grouped into **Large, Medium, and Small** categories to compare performance more effectively.

### Key Findings
- **Large stores generated the highest average sales**
- **Medium stores showed moderate performance**
- **Small stores recorded the lowest average sales**

### Business Insight
Store size demonstrated a strong relationship with sales performance, with larger stores consistently outperforming smaller formats.

---

## Overall Conclusion

The analysis revealed significant variation in store performance across locations, store types, and store sizes.

### Major Findings
- **Store 20 emerged as the strongest-performing store**
- **Type A stores consistently outperformed other store categories**
- **Large stores generated the highest average sales**
- Store size showed a strong positive relationship with revenue performance

Overall, store performance appears to be influenced by both **store format and size**, highlighting important considerations for operational planning, expansion, and investment decisions.
