# Hypotheses

After understanding the business problem and identifying the key business questions, the next step was to develop hypotheses.

A hypothesis is an **educated assumption** about a possible relationship in the data that will later be tested through analysis.

The purpose of hypotheses is to guide analysis by creating assumptions that can either be:

- Supported by data ✅  
- Rejected by data ❌  

Both outcomes are important because they help improve business understanding.

In this project, hypotheses were developed based on the business questions and MECE framework.


## 1. Store Characteristics

### H1: Larger stores may generate higher weekly sales.

It is expected that larger stores may generate stronger sales performance because they are likely to have more inventory capacity and serve more customers.

### H2: Store type may influence sales performance.

It is expected that differences in store type (`A`, `B`, `C`) may influence weekly sales performance.


## 2. Economic Conditions

### H3: Higher unemployment may negatively influence retail sales performance.

It is expected that higher unemployment may reduce customer spending power, which could influence weekly sales performance.

### H4: Higher CPI may influence sales performance.

It is expected that increases in CPI (inflation) may reduce customer purchasing power and influence weekly sales performance.

### H5: Higher fuel prices may influence retail sales performance.

It is expected that rising fuel prices may reduce customer travel and non-essential spending, which could influence retail sales performance.


## 3. Seasonal Effects

### H6: Holiday weeks may generate higher sales than non-holiday weeks.

It is expected that holiday periods may encourage customer spending and improve weekly sales performance.

### H7: Retail sales may display seasonal patterns over time.

It is expected that some weeks or periods may consistently generate stronger sales than others.


## 4. Promotions

### H8: Promotional markdowns may improve sales performance.

It is expected that promotional markdown campaigns may increase weekly sales.

### H9: Promotions may be more effective during holiday periods.

It is expected that markdowns may generate stronger results when combined with holiday shopping periods.


## 5. Department Performance

### H10: Certain departments may consistently outperform others.

It is expected that some departments may generate stronger and more consistent sales performance than others.


## Why Hypotheses Matter

Hypotheses help make sure analysis is conducted with purpose.

Instead of exploring data randomly, SQL analysis is used to test assumptions and determine whether expected relationships are supported by evidence.
