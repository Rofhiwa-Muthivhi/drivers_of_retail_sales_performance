## **MECE Framework**

MECE stands for: `Mutually Exclusive` `Collectively Exhaustive`

The MECE framework is a way of breaking down a large business problem into smaller, well-organized sections. Each section focuses on a different part of the problem without overlapping with the others, while together they cover everything that needs to be analysed.

In this project, I used the MECE framework to organize the business questions into separate categories. This made the analysis more structured, easier to follow, and ensured that each question focused on a specific business area. Using this approach also helped make sure that no important factors affecting retail sales performance were left out of the analysis.

To better investigate the business problem, the following questions were developed using the MECE framework. 
Organizing the analysis in this way created a logical flow, allowing each question to focus on a different aspect of retail sales performance while ensuring that all key areas of the business problem were covered:

#### Store Characteristics
1) How does weekly sales performance vary across individual stores?
2) Does store size influence weekly sales performance?
3) How does weekly sales performance differ across store types (A, B, and C)?
4) Which stores consistently outperform or underperform other stores of the same type? 

#### Seasonal Effects
5) Do holiday weeks generate higher weekly sales than non-holiday weeks?
6) How does weekly sales performance vary across months and years?
7) How does weekly temperature influence departmental sales performance?
8) Which departments are most sensitive to changes in weekly temperature?

#### Promotions
9) Do markdown promotions improve weekly sales performance?
10) Which markdown categories have the strongest relationship with weekly sales?
11) Which departments benefit the most from promotional markdowns?

#### Economic Conditions
12) How do unemployment levels influence weekly sales performance?
13) How does the Consumer Price Index (CPI) relate to weekly sales performance?
14) Does fuel price influence customer purchasing behaviour and weekly sales?

#### Department Performance
15) How does weekly sales performance vary across departments?
16) Which departments consistently outperform or underperform?
17) Which departments experience the greatest fluctuations in weekly sales over time?
